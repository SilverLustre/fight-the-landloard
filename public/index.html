A QUICK INTRODUCTION

One landlord.
Two unlikely allies.

Everyone gets 17 cards. Bid 1, 2, or 3 for the landlord role, or pass. Each player bids once; the highest bidder gets three bonus cards and leads. A bid of 3 ends bidding. If everyone passes, we redeal.

The landlord plays alone. The other two players are a team. Empty your hand to win for your side.

Play something stronger.

Match the type and length of the last play with a higher rank, or pass. After two passes, the last player leads anything. Rank order: 3–10, J, Q, K, A, 2, low joker, high joker.

Play singles, pairs, triples (alone or with a single/pair), straights (5+), consecutive pairs (3+), consecutive triples (2+, with optional single/pair wings), or four-of-a-kind with two single/pair wings. Runs cannot contain 2s or jokers. Wings must use distinct ranks outside the core.

A four-of-a-kind bomb beats ordinary combinations. Both jokers together—the rocket—beat everything. Bombs and rockets double the score multiplier. Spring doubles it again when the losing farmers never play, or the losing landlord plays only once.

The stake is the winning bid × multiplier. The landlord gains/loses twice the stake; each farmer loses/gains one stake. Scores are casual room points, not money.

Turns last 30 seconds. On timeout, the server bids or plays for you. Disconnected players keep their seat; reload to reconnect. Player-hosted rooms end if the hosting server stops.
